# Ed25519 verification gas

Measured with `bench/ed25519.sh` (forge gas snapshots, local revm, standard EVM gas schedule) on an
Apple M4 Pro, macOS 15.1 (Darwin 24.1.0), forge 1.5.1. These are not Tempo measurements. Tempo
prices storage differently, but these paths only compute, and it caps one transaction at 30M gas.

| Operation | Gas |
|---|---|
| `LibEd25519.verify`, one 185-byte acknowledgment message (the crate's framed `VectorSendBody` and predecessor) | 949,348 |
| `LibEd25519.isStrictKey` (canonical, non-identity, prime order) | 327,584 |

Most of the cost is the 253-step joint double-and-add in extended twisted-Edwards coordinates and
the vendored Solidity SHA-512. No windowing or precomputed tables are used yet.

## Challenge totals

Execution gas of one `challenge` call (forge gas snapshots in `contracts/snapshots/Challenges.json`,
same machine). Calldata is extra: 16 gas per nonzero byte on the standard schedule.

| Challenge | Gas |
|---|---|
| AckFork, crate fixture, proven (2 strict payer keys, 2 operator signatures) | 2,646,708 |
| HigherAckDebit, crate fixture, proven (1 strict payer key, payer and operator signatures, 1-row opening) | 2,348,426 |
| HigherAckDebit, absent payer, two neighbour rows, proven | 2,973,857 |
| Largest structurally valid evidence (8,225 bytes: two neighbour rows, 244-digest opening), rejected | 2,939,613 (`test_GasChallengeWorstCase`) |

Worst case against Tempo's 30M per-transaction cap: the bounded decode admits at most three strict
keys (payer and two neighbour rows, about 0.33M each), two signature checks (about 0.95M each), and
one opening of at most 244 digests (one SHA-256 precompile call per digest). With the 8,225-byte
evidence plus the 632-byte header witness as calldata (about 0.14M) and the 21,000 intrinsic gas,
the largest challenge costs roughly 3.1M gas, about a tenth of the cap. Ed25519 cost does not make
challenges infeasible on Tempo. Tempo-specific pricing is still unmeasured; P2.3 runs these on
Moderato.
